Question 2: Indian Government Web Portals

A curated directory of five official central digital governance platforms of India.

1. National Portal of India

Official URL: https://www.india.gov.in

Purpose & Features: Serves as the single-window access point to all information and electronic services provided by various state and central government departments in India. Developed and hosted by the National Informatics Centre (NIC).

2. MyGov India (Meri Sarkar)

Official URL: https://www.mygov.in

Purpose & Features: An innovative citizen engagement platform launched by the Government of India to foster active citizen participation in governance, policy formulation, quizzes, surveys, and national initiatives.

3. UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India)

Official URL: https://uidai.gov.in

Purpose & Features: Official portal for the Aadhaar digital identity program. Enables Indian citizens to download electronic Aadhaar (e-Aadhaar), verify identities, schedule enrollment center appointments, and update personal demographic data online.

4. Income Tax e-Filing Portal

Official URL: https://www.incometax.gov.in

Purpose & Features: The official portal maintained by the Ministry of Finance for digital submission of annual Income Tax Returns (ITR), tracking refund status, online PAN application, and Aadhaar-PAN linkage verification.

5. Digital India Portal

Official URL: https://www.digitalindia.gov.in

Purpose & Features: Flagship government portal detailing initiatives aimed at transforming India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy through broadband highway expansion, public cloud, and digital literacy.
